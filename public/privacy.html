1. The short version

  • To play online you sign in with Steam, or with an account (email and password, or Discord). We store your account, your characters, and your progress so the world is there when you return.
  • Chat, character names, guilds, trades, and leaderboards are visible to other players. Treat what you type or name as public.
  • We use technical data such as your IP address to run the servers, keep accounts secure, and stop cheating and abuse.
  • Analytics are off unless you turn them on. The Game asks once, on first launch, and you can change your answer at any time in Options. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any third-party analytics or advertising company.
  • You can export your data and delete your account.

2. Information we collect

Steam sign-in. When you sign in with Steam, Steam sends us a sign-in ticket that we verify with Valve. We receive and store your Steam ID (a number that identifies your Steam account) and link it to your Wyndlewood account. We do not receive your Steam password or payment details. Your Steam purchases, refunds, and Steam profile are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. If we enable Steam achievements, we send achievement unlocks for your Steam ID to Steam.

Account information. If you create an account without Steam we collect a username, a password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash, never in readable form), and an email address used for sign-in, password reset, and important account messages. If you sign in with Discord we receive your Discord user ID, username, and avatar. If you choose to receive news by email we record that choice and you can withdraw it at any time.

Characters and gameplay. Character names, class, appearance, level, items, currency, quests, housing, achievements (the Book of Deeds), position in the world, and similar game state, so your progress is saved.

Chat and social activity. Messages you send in chat channels, whispers, guild and party activity, mail, trades, friend lists, and reports you file or that are filed about you. Chat is delivered to other players and stored for moderation, safety, and abuse prevention.

Agreement records. The version of the End User License Agreement you accepted and when you accepted it, stored with your account, so we can show that the current agreement was accepted.

Technical and security data. IP address, session tokens (which expire), connection times, client version, operating system and device type, error reports the Game sends when it crashes or hits an error, and, if you file one, a bug report with the details you include. We use IP addresses to rate-limit sign-in attempts, detect cheating, multi-account abuse, and gold selling, and enforce bans. Crash dumps from the desktop app stay on your computer unless a crash upload address has been configured for your build; when one is, the dump contains only process and version information.

Optional analytics (off by default). Only if you choose "Share" in the first-launch prompt or turn on "Share Usage and Performance Data" in Options, the Game sends two kinds of data to our own servers, never to any other company:

  1. Coarse counters. Each event carries only an event name, whether you play on the desktop app or in a browser, and a rough frame-rate bucket (for example "40 to 60 frames per second"). The event names are exactly: session_start (your first world entry in a session), world_enter (each world entry), play_interval (one more five minutes of play), and session_end (the Game is closing). These events carry no account, character, device identifier, or IP-derived location, and we store no rows for them: our servers only add them to aggregate counters.
  2. Performance reports. Every few minutes during play, a report about frame times, loading times, graphics settings, and your graphics hardware (for example GPU family, memory size, and power mode), linked to your session and account so we can investigate performance problems you report.

If you turn analytics off, nothing more is sent from that moment. Turning it off does not change anything else about the Game.

Cookies and local storage. The Game and the Site use local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings, your analytics choice, and your local copy of the agreement you accepted. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Community channels. Our Discord server and Steam community pages are run on those platforms, under their own privacy policies.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information such as health data or precise location.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the Game: your account, characters, multiplayer features, and saves.
  • Keep the Service secure and fair: prevent cheating, fraud, abuse, and harassment; moderate content; enforce the Terms of Service and the Code of Conduct.
  • Fix bugs, crashes, and performance problems.
  • Understand how many people play and how well the Game runs, only from the optional analytics you opted in to.
  • Provide support and send important messages about your account or the Service (for example a planned wipe or a change to these documents).
  • Meet legal obligations and answer lawful requests.
