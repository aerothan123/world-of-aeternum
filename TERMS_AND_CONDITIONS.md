# TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS (superseded)

This file held the upstream World of ClaudeCraft project's legal text, which
named its operator and services that Wyndlewood does not use. It no longer
applies to Wyndlewood.

Wyndlewood's legal documents are drafts awaiting lawyer review, under
`docs/legal/`: the End User License Agreement (`eula.md`), the Terms of
Service (`terms-of-service.md`), the Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.md`), the
Code of Conduct (`code-of-conduct.md`), and the Early Access and Refunds note
(`early-access-and-refunds.md`). The player-facing pages are
`public/eula.html`, `public/terms.html`, and `public/privacy.html`.
