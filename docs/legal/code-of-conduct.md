# Wyndlewood Code of Conduct (players)

> **DRAFT TEMPLATE.** Review with a lawyer and the moderation team before
> publishing. Values in square brackets are placeholders.

Wyndlewood is a shared world. This Code applies everywhere in the game
(chat, whispers, mail, guilds, names, housing, trades) and in our official
community spaces such as the Discord server and the Steam forums.

## Be kind

- Treat other players with respect. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, bullying, stalking, threats, or hate speech, including
  attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender
  identity, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
- No sexual content involving or directed at minors, ever. We report it to the
  authorities.
- No sexually explicit, gory, or shock content, and no spamming or flooding.
- Do not share anyone's personal information (doxxing), and protect your own.

## Play fair

- No cheats, hacks, bots, macros that play for you, or modified clients that
  give an advantage.
- Do not exploit bugs. Report them through the in-game bug report.
- One person per account; no account sharing, buying, or selling.
- No real-money trading of items, currency, characters, or services, and no
  advertising for it.
- Do not impersonate staff, other players, or the developers.

## Names and creations

Character, guild, and house names, and anything you build or write, must not be
offensive, sexual, hateful, or impersonate someone, and must not use other
people's trademarks in a misleading way.

## Reporting

Use the in-game report option on a player or message, or write to
[SUPPORT EMAIL]. Reports are confidential. Do not take matters into your own
hands.

## What happens if the Code is broken

Depending on how serious it is: a warning, a chat mute, a rename, removal of
content, rollback of gains, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban.
Cheating, real-money trading, and threats usually lead straight to a
suspension or ban. You can appeal by writing to [SUPPORT EMAIL]; a different
moderator reviews appeals.
