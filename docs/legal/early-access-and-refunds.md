Wyndlewood is in Early Access: it is playable, but unfinished. We are adding
content, fixing bugs, and changing balance as we go, guided by player feedback.
Expect bugs, rough edges, server restarts, and changes to features you use.
Buy it for what it is today, not for promises about the future.

## Progress and the one planned wipe

Your characters and progress are kept through Early Access. We plan ONE
progress reset when the game reaches full launch (version 1.0). Cosmetics and
Book of Deeds records are kept through that reset. We will announce the date
well in advance in the game, on the Steam store page, and on Discord. We may
also need to roll back recent data to fix a serious bug or exploit; we will
explain when that happens.

## Refunds

Wyndlewood is sold on Steam, so refunds go through Steam's refund policy
(currently, a request within 14 days of purchase with less than 2 hours of
playtime is normally granted; see
https://store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds/). We do not sell the game or
anything inside the Steam build outside Steam, and we cannot issue refunds
ourselves. If a refund is granted, the game is removed from your Steam library;
your Wyndlewood account and characters stay until you ask us to delete them.
