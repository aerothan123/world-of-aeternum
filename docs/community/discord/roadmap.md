**Wyndlewood Roadmap**

What we are building, in rough order. No dates yet: we will share them when we are confident in them.

**In Progress**
- **Eastbrook rebuilt:** a crafting yard with thirteen benches, Market Row with the trading post and storage, a stable at the north gate, and ten homes to own, all rebuilt at higher detail.
- **Chapter One, "The Shore That Remembers":** 28 quests from the Proving Shore to Eastbrook, plus the Eastbrook quest board with rotating daily work.
- **The four friendly factions:** Lanternwardens, Hearthwright Compact, Bracken Circle and Gilt Measure, with reputation earned by what you do.
- **New characters:** a chunky hand-painted style for players and townsfolk, with armor that shows every piece you wear.
- **Creatures:** wildlife and new enemies for the early zones.
- **Weapons:** new swords, axes, staves, bows and shields, with looks that improve as your gear does.
- **Homes and furniture:** a full furniture catalogue to craft and decorate with.
- **A painted interface** to match the world.
- **Sound and music:** ambient sound for every region, and a soundtrack of regional themes and tavern songs.
- **Environment polish:** painted water, cliffs, snow and marsh.

**Next**
- **A bigger world:** new lands to the south and west, larger zones, and travel by cart and ferry between towns.
- **The Hushmist:** the misty edge of the known world, which pulls back as new chapters arrive.
- **Life skills as a path to level 20:** work orders, town restoration projects and crafting progression.
- **Gear durability and repair**, and a first mount earlier in your journey.
- **One home per account**, shared by all your characters.

**Later**
- **Playtests** for Discord members, in waves.
- **Steam Early Access** on PC.
- **New chapters**, each raising the level cap and opening new lands.
- **A PvP arena or PvP zone.** PvP stays opt-in at launch.
