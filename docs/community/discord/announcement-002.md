**Dev update: a bigger world, our own score, and new heroes**

It has been a busy few days in Wyndlewood. Here is what the team has been building.

**The world grew**
- Seven new zones across the Low Reaches and the West March, from the salt flats of the Saltmeads to the lantern-lit hills of Wardmere
- A causeway road south from Eastbrook, new hub towns with quest boards and Ravenpost mailboxes, and landmarks to find along the way: wind pumps, old mine engine houses, lookouts and a broken march wall
- No loading screens between zones

**Our own music**
The game now has its own original score: every region by day and night, every town, dungeons, boss fights, and six songs you will hear in taverns and on the road. Two tracks are attached below: the title theme and "Keep to the Road".

**Named weapons**
Sixty-four named weapons now have their own hand-made looks, from Hearthbrand and Rimelight to Starfall and Skyrender.

**Chapter Two is taking shape**
Twenty-six new quests across three new acts: cider country in the Applewold, fires that burn with no fuel on the Kindlemoor, and bells heard at low tide along the Chalkbarrow Coast.

Everything here is work in progress and will keep changing. Tell us what you think in #feedback.
