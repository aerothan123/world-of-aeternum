**The Shore That Remembers**

Long before kingdoms, the world was wild, and the first settlers learned that the land answered how it was treated. Families who honored the forest found shelter beneath it. Villages that cared for their rivers never went hungry. Over generations these understandings became a quiet bond between people and land: the Hearthbinding, the promises that held the world together.

That was the Age of First Hearths.

Now the old promises are fraying. Animals behave strangely. Travelers vanish on familiar roads. Ruins that slept for generations are waking. Most villagers call it superstition. Some remember the old stories, and an old cautionary verse about a ruler who would not keep his word.

You arrive by ferry, an ordinary traveler looking for a fresh start. Perhaps the beacon on the headland was not supposed to lead your boat onto the rocks.
