**Welcome to Wyndlewood**

Rolling green hills beneath golden skies. Crooked cottages and turning watermills. Taverns that fill with laughter as the sun goes down behind the mountains.

And beyond the warmth of the village lanterns, something is beginning to stir.

Wyndlewood is a hand-painted fantasy MMO about making a home in a world worth living in. Fight, gather, craft, build a house, open a shop, fish by the river at dusk, or follow the strange signs that something old has woken up. Every adventure begins somewhere. In Wyndlewood, it begins with a ferry.
