**Patch Notes: Development Build, November 4**

This is the first development build of the new Wyndlewood look. Everything below is in the current internal build.

**The World**
- New painted look across the whole world: softer light, warmer colour, and a gentle blue haze toward the horizon.
- Hand-painted ground textures for meadows, paths, sand, cliffs and rock.
- Trees rebuilt from hundreds of painted leaf clusters instead of solid shapes. Darker leaves sit inside the crown and lighter ones at the edges, and the whole crown sways in the wind.
- Pines have needle sprays, dead trees have fine twigs, and bushes and flowering shrubs got the same leafy treatment.
- Grass is now made of narrow pointed blades of mixed length, with flower patches mixed in.
- Trees between you and the camera fade out so they no longer block your view.
- Distant trees use the new look too.

**Gathering**
- New mining sounds: a solid pick striking stone, with the ring echoing across the valley.
- New woodcutting sounds: heavy axe strikes, plus a creak and crash when a tree comes down.
- Strikes echo differently in the open, near cliffs, in forests and in caves.

**Performance**
- The new trees draw fewer triangles than the old ones, so busy forest and meadow views run slightly faster than before.

**Known Issues**
- A few long willow-style fronds can hang as streaks at the edge of a tree crown up close.
- Shadowed sides of some rocks look too blue.
