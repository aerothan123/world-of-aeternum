**The Hearth Rules**

Long ago the people of Wyndlewood kept a simple promise with the land: take care of what takes care of you. We ask the same here.

1. **Be kind.** No harassment, hate, slurs, or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. **Keep it safe for everyone.** No NSFW, gore, or shock content.
3. **No spam or self-promotion** without a moderator's OK.
4. **Spoilers in spoiler tags.** Use ||like this|| for story and quest details.
5. **Bugs go to #bug-reports**, ideas to #suggestions, everything else to #feedback.
6. **No cheating, exploits, or real-money trading** of in-game goods or accounts. Found an exploit? Report it privately to a moderator.
7. **Respect the playtest.** Do not share private builds or leak unreleased content.
8. **Moderators have the final word.** If something feels wrong, message a moderator.

Breaking the rules may lead to a warning, a mute, or a ban.
