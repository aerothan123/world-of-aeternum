**Playtesting Wyndlewood**

Wyndlewood is in active development and heading toward Steam Early Access on PC.

**What testers will help with**
- Chapter One: the Proving Shore and the town of Eastbrook (levels 1 to 4)
- Questing, gathering, crafting, and the new Eastbrook quest board
- Housing: your first home, storage, and furniture
- Performance on different PCs

**How it will work**
- Playtest invites go out in waves through Discord
- Testers get the **Playtester** role and access to private test channels
- Report bugs in #bug-reports with what you did, what you expected, and what happened (screenshots help)

Sign-ups open soon. Keep an eye on #announcements.
