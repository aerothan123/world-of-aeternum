WYNDLEWOOD
CHAPTER ONE: THE SHORE THAT REMEMBERS
Levels 1 to 4

The 28 quests contain 12 main quests and 12 side stories: eight standalone quests and four two-part chains. The 12 daily templates follow separately.

Additional townsfolk: Tobbin Wickerby, a fisherman; Pella Nookwright, a carpenter; Della Brindlepot, a gardener; and Holven Marr, a gravekeeper.

Story notes are designer-only. The player discovers evidence of coordinated wrongdoing, but does not learn the Pale Accord's name, leadership, or full purpose.

========================================
THE PROVING SHORE
========================================

01

Title: An Arrival Without a Ticket
Type: main
Level: 1
Giver and turn-in NPC: Fenlow, beside the beached skiff on the Proving Shore's southern beach.
Prerequisite quest: none
Objectives: Collect 3 Dry Supply Bundles from wreckage along the southern beach; inspect the Broken Lifeboat; return to Fenlow.
Offer text: "You're breathing, which puts you ahead of your luggage. Gather anything dry from that wreckage while I fetch a blanket. We can discuss your unusual choice of landing place once your teeth stop chattering."
Progress text: "Have you found enough dry supplies to make yourself useful rather than merely damp?"
Completion text: "There's bread in that bundle and a blanket without too many holes. Sit a moment, then we'll work out what brought your boat onto the rocks."
Reward faction: Lanternwardens
Story note: The wreck introduces the consequences of a tampered shore signal without revealing who altered it.

02

Title: The Beacon That Lied
Type: main
Level: 1
Giver and turn-in NPC: Fenlow, outside the ferry hut on the Proving Shore.
Prerequisite quest: An Arrival Without a Ticket
Objectives: Kill 4 Bristlejaw Shore Rats on the northern headland path; inspect the Northern Beacon; collect 2 Black Wick Knots from its opened lantern housing.
Offer text: "That beacon should have guided your boat clear of the rocks, not invited it onto them. Someone's been up there, and the rats along the path have started biting anything with a shadow. Have a look inside the lantern, but don't touch anything still burning."
Progress text: "What was inside the beacon that shouldn't have been?"
Completion text: "That cord is cold enough to numb my fingers, yet the lantern was lit. I'll wrap it up for Eastbrook's priest, because I prefer my mysteries somewhere other than aboard my boat."
Reward faction: Lanternwardens
Story note: A Pale Accord cell is testing treated cord that also appears in later grave rituals.

03

Type: side
Level: 1
Giver and turn-in NPC: Tobbin Wickerby, at the Proving Shore fishing jetty.
Prerequisite quest: An Arrival Without a Ticket

Offer text: "Before we discuss fishing, you should understand that the fish consider themselves the injured party. Borrow my spare rod and catch a few minnows for the keep-net. Gently, please, because we're studying them rather than inviting them to supper."
Progress text: "Have the fish agreed to participate?"
Completion text: "Four healthy minnows, and not one signed complaint. We'll release them after I've checked their scales, which is more courtesy than they showed my last sandwich."
Reward faction: Bracken Circle
Story note: standalone

12 REPEATABLE DAILY TEMPLATES
========================================

Daily rules: Variables in braces are filled from the permitted choices in each Objectives field. Select one matching target and location pair per posting. Daily counts never include named quest characters, protected trees, unique story objects, or dungeon enemies.

Completing "Someone to Vouch for You" introduces the board. Individual postings still require their listed level. All daily objectives are available without waiting for a particular time of day.

Type: daily
Level: 2

Prerequisite quest: Someone to Vouch for You
Objectives: Kill {COUNT} {TARGET} at {LOCATION}; report to Corren. Count: 4 to 6. Target/location pairs: Culvert Rats at the town drainage ditch, or Ditch Beetles beside the western roadside.
